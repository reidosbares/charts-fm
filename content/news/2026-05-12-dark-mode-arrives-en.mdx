## Dark mode

This is the big one. ChartsFM now has a proper, end-to-end **dark mode**. Every page, every modal, every card, every chart bubble has been swept for dark variants.

![The ChartsFM dashboard in dark mode, with a featured track carousel, weekly listening stats, top artists/tracks/albums and a recent activity panel.](/news/2026-05-12-dark-mode.png)

There are three options:

- **Light**: what you've been looking at this whole time
- **Dark**: the new one
- **System**: follows your OS preference, so it auto-flips when your phone or laptop does

You can change it in two places:

- The **sun/moon/system icon** in the navbar (top right on desktop, inside the menu on mobile)
- The new **Appearance** section in your user settings

Your choice persists across sessions, and logged-in users get it mirrored to their account so it carries to new devices.

## Deep-dive page polish

The artist/track/album drill-down pages got a visual pass:

- The timeline bubbles were slightly reworked for a more uniform look.
- All dates on the timeline now respect your locale, so Portuguese readers stop seeing English-formatted dates

## Charts page

- Position, plays, and VS changes since last week now show up as red/green change badges next to each entry
- Position indicators swapped from emoji to FontAwesome icons, looks cleaner and renders the same on every platform
- Fixed the week-selector calendar, finally. It now renders correctly instead of being clipped by the surrounding sidebar

## Records and awards

- Fixed a bug where member award rankings could include entries from other groups
- Peak weekly records now display the chart position where the record was set
- VS values on award entries now keep their decimals (rounding to integers was hiding close calls)

## Other things

- **Trends streaks** now count any chart appearance. Previously, they only counted top-10 appearances. The group's obsession still only tracks current top 10 entries.
- Performance: **Artist images** are cached in our CDN, so they load faster and stop disappearing when Wikimedia rate-limits us.
- The **Discover page** got a perf pass: metadata queries are batched and filtering and sorting now happen in the database instead of after the fact.

That's it for today. Enjoy the dark mode.
